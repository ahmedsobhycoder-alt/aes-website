# Editorial plan

## The honest diagnosis

The technical SEO on this site is now in good shape. **Content depth is the
binding constraint**, and no amount of further markup will change that.

Two specific gaps:

1. **Project pages carry 2–3 sentences each.** `challenge`, `approach`,
   `outcome` — roughly 60–80 words. Competing studio case studies run 400–800.
2. **No page owns "restaurant interior design Egypt"** despite AES having nine
   F&B projects. This is the highest-value gap on the site.

Nothing below should be published without verified AES source material. Do not
mass-generate articles.

---

## Priority 1 — An F&B service page

The single highest-return piece of work.

- **Primary query:** restaurant interior design Egypt
- **Supporting:** F&B design Egypt, hospitality interior design Egypt, food court design, restaurant fit-out Cairo
- **Evidence available now:** Ozel, Nuwa, Cassandra Food Court, Big Pappy,
  Bad Ziggy, Blues, Savoy, Sababa, Antika Beirut — nine real projects
- **Contributor:** both founders

  service-flow or kitchen-adjacency decisions on a named project
- **Links in:** `/services`, `/work`, both founder pages, homepage services grid
- **Links out:** the nine project pages
- **CTA:** Start a Project
- **Photography:** already available

## Priority 2 — Deepen the top 6 project pages

Target Ozel, Nuwa, Rixance, Cassandra Food Court, Hyde Park, Aaly Elmaqam
(strongest imagery, most search interest). Expand each to 300–500 words using
only verified facts:

- The brief and the real constraint (site, budget, programme, timeline)
- The spatial move that resolved it — the actual decision, not adjectives
- Material palette and *why* those materials (durability, acoustics, maintenance)
- Lighting strategy across dayparts
- Execution or coordination challenges
- Outcome in concrete terms

**Do not fabricate** area, budget, client name, year, covers, or performance
figures. If AES cannot confirm a fact, omit it — a shorter true page beats a
longer invented one.

Also: give each page a distinct opening paragraph. Repeating one template across
19 projects reads as boilerplate to both readers and classifiers.

For each, record before writing: search intent, primary query, contributor,
project evidence, internal link target, CTA, and whether original photography
exists. If photography does not exist, the article waits.

## Voice

Keep the existing editorial register. The current copy — *"Execution is where
design is either protected or lost"* — is specific, confident and true. That is
the standard.

Banned phrasing, regardless of SEO value:

- "We turn dreams into reality"
- "Best interior design company"
- "World-class solutions"
- "Luxury redefined"

Claims like "leading" or "award-winning" require a verifiable source. Without
one, they are both a trust problem and, under Google's spam policies, a quality
signal working against you.

## Measurement

Track per published piece, at 30 and 90 days: impressions, clicks, average
position for the primary query, and assisted conversions to `/contact`. If a
piece produces nothing after 90 days, revise it rather than publishing another.
